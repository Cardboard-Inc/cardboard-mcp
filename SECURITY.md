# Security

To report a security problem in Cardboard's MCP server, its sign-in, or the files in this repo, email
[support@cardboard.ai](mailto:support@cardboard.ai) with "Security" in the subject. Please don't open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it could let someone do. We'll reply to confirm we have it, and
keep you posted while we fix it.

Please test only with your own Cardboard account, and don't access or change other people's data.
